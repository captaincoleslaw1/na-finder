🏠 NA Finder

NA: No Address. Letter has a name but no address, or the wrong one? Type the name, check the county owner records, then let the web miner try. Covers your whole delivery area: 07712, Allenhurst (07711) and Deal (07723). It also runs backwards: type an address, get the owner on record.

1. Who is the letter for?

The wrong address helps in two ways: the miner will not suggest it back to you, and a near-miss (right street, wrong number) is a clue, not a dead end.

One tap. First it searches the county owner records for all 7 of your towns and ZIP-confirms the matches, then it hunts the web (Bing + DuckDuckGo). Every suggestion shows its source and never fills anything in on its own.

1. What address do you need the owner for?

One tap. It walks that street in the county tax records for all 7 of your towns. The current owner shows in green; earlier owners from the deed/sales records show below in yellow. Owners only — a renter will not show up here.

Found it? It goes here

Line up just the address block in the yellow box. It fills the name, plus the printed address as the wrong address if there is one.

Typing is the fast path. The camera stays off until you ask for it.

2. County owner records (OPRS) — the manual route ▾ tap to open

Monmouth County's tax records list every property owner by town. Owners only, so renters will not show up here — the Suggest button in step 1 covers a lot of those. There is no ZIP search, so you check town by town and keep only results in your ZIPs: 07712, 07711 and 07723 (the same town can have other ZIPs too).

Owner Name to type into OPRS (their format is LAST, FIRST):

— type a name above —
  1. In OPRS: Database = Current Owners, County = Monmouth
  2. Pick the District FIRST, then paste the Owner Name, then Submit. Changing the town can wipe the name and dump the whole town on you.
  3. Read the Location column. Tap More Info to confirm the ZIP is one of yours: 07712, 07711 or 07723.
  4. Check each town off below as you finish it.

Working from an address instead of a name? Leave Owner Name blank and put just the street name — no house number, no Rd/St — in the Location field. The county answers a bare street name, then you find your number in the list.

Found one? Scroll back up to step 1 and put it in the "Found it?" fields — or use Copy Complete Address up there once it is filled in.

3. Still stuck? Search the web by hand

The automatic Suggest hunt lives up in step 1. If it and OPRS both came up empty, these are the same searches done manually — people-search sites cover renters and recent movers that the tax records miss.

Once a name is typed above, manual search buttons appear here, already filled in.

Broker info can be months out of date, so sanity-check an address before you use it. A wrong delivery is worse than no delivery. Buttons dim once tapped, so you can keep your place working down the list.

Privacy: a photo is read right here on your phone. Nothing is uploaded or stored. The searches open on the sites themselves, same as typing them in by hand.
